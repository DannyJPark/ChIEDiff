WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
Protonation is NOT performed by one shared tool, contrary to the original plan (§3.4).
PoseCheck shells out to `hydride` (it hardcodes that command and ignores its own reduce_path
argument); PLIP delegates to openbabel. The two therefore disagree on absolute interaction
counts. This is acceptable because within each instrument every model gets identical treatment,
so model-vs-model comparison is valid -- but absolute counts must never be compared ACROSS the
two instruments, and that is only auditable if the versions are written down.

Do not "fix" this by switching PoseCheck to reduce: it would invalidate the 7 model tags of
PoseCheck results already computed.

local patches that affect measured values
=====================================================================
  scripts/plip_interactions.py   remap_chains(): rewrites PDB col 22 from the (chainID, segID)
                                 pair. Without it, 11 of 100 NATIVE pockets silently score (0,0).
                                 Also selects interaction_sets by the 'LIG:Z:1' key, because
                                 receptors containing ions otherwise yield the ion's empty set.
  scripts/eval_posecheck.py      _patch_prolif_vdwradii(): supplies vdW radii for CO/CD/NA/FE/ZN/
                                 MG/MN/CA/CU/NI/K, and shifts negative resids to >=1 (prolif casts
                                 resids to uint32 and overflows). Without it a receptor holding
                                 any of these loses its whole pocket.
  scripts/eval_export_sdf.py     load_grouped(): places flat .pt sets at their CANONICAL pocket
                                 index rather than renumbering by order of appearance, which had
                                 paired 77 PIDiff pockets with the wrong receptor.
